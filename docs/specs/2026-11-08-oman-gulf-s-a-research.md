# Oman Gulf Company S.A.O.C — research brief

**Date:** 2026-11-08
**Researched for:** mock-designer phase of the mock-builder pipeline

## Confirmed facts
Only one public source (a directory profile) was found for this company. Treat all facts below as single-sourced.

- Oman-based company engaged in civil engineering contracting, earthmoving projects and transport works. Source: Zawya company profile, https://www.zawya.com/company/5000833868/oman-gulf
- Incorporated in Oman, incorporation date listed as 1974-01-01. Source: Zawya profile (same URL). The date looks like a placeholder, so treat the year as approximate.
- Zawya classifies the sector as energy / fossil fuels. Source: Zawya profile.
- Listed clients are Petroleum Development Oman (PDO), Japex Oman, Philips Petroleum 66, ELF and ARCO Oman. Source: Zawya profile.
- Locations are a regional office in Salalah, a workshop in Thumrait and a facility in Ghala, Muscat. P.O. Box 223. Source: Zawya profile.
- Zawya states no employee count, revenue, officers, parent or subsidiaries. Source: Zawya profile (fields not stated).
- Gludo's directory lists it as a construction company in Zufar with a Salalah address. Source: https://gludo.org/en/business-directory/3076-oman-gulf-company-saoc (directory only, rating claims unverified).
- No official website, news coverage or annual report was found in the searches run.

## Group / entity structure
Not found. No public source names a parent, subsidiaries or a VAT group. No indication of a shared Oman VAT Group TRN. The company appears to be a standalone SAOC, but this is an absence of evidence, not a confirmed fact.

Note on name confusion. Searches returned similarly named but unrelated entities (Oman Oil Company S.A.O.C., Oman Gas Company SAOC, Oman Oil Marketing Company SAOG, Gulf Agency Company (Oman) L.L.C.). Nothing found links them to Oman Gulf Company.

## ERP / systems signal
No public signal found. ERP search results concerned other Omani firms and are not relevant.

## Shape recommendation (non-binding)
This looks like a single-entity case: one SAOC civil/earthmoving/transport contractor with a Salalah regional office, a Thumrait workshop and a Muscat facility, serving mainly oil and gas operators. Locations are sites of one entity, not separate legal entities. Confidence is medium-low. The evidence is a single thin directory profile, and the absence of any group evidence is not proof that none exists. The designer should not model a VAT group or multi-tenant setup unless there is a separate reason.

## Gaps / assumptions the designer will need to fill
- Legal entity details: TRN, CR number, exact registered address, and whether the 1974 date is real.
- Ownership: parent, shareholders and any subsidiaries.
- Scale: headcount, revenue, fleet size, number of active projects, and invoice and transaction volumes.
- ERP: product, version and hosting. Nothing is known publicly.
- Counterparties: current client list and their invoicing requirements (PDO and others), beyond the legacy client names on Zawya.
- People: finance and IT contacts.
- Business flows: contract billing model (milestone or measurement-based, retention, subcontractor and equipment-hire invoicing), VAT treatment, and any export or zero-rated supplies.
- Whether the company is still active under this name, given the dated client list (Philips Petroleum 66, ELF, ARCO).
